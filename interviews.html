🎤 Interview prep

Walk into your interview prepared.

Use these structures to practice. Adapt every answer to your real experience.

Tell me about yourself.

Use: present → past → strengths → why this role. Keep it around 45–90 seconds and focus on experience relevant to the job.

Why do you want this job?

Connect your skills to the role and explain why the company's work interests you. Avoid making salary your only reason.

What is your biggest strength?

Choose one strength and prove it with a short real example. For customer-facing work, communication, reliability and problem-solving can be useful examples when they are genuinely true.

What is your weakness?

Choose a genuine but manageable weakness and explain what you are doing to improve it.

Why should we hire you?

Give three points: relevant experience, attitude/reliability, and the value you can bring to the team.

Role-specific preparation

Customer Service

Practice difficult customers, communication, complaint handling and teamwork.

Hospitality

Practice guest service, busy shifts, hygiene, teamwork and handling complaints.

Office & Admin

Practice organization, computer skills, prioritization, accuracy and communication.
