Practical guides for job seekers

Short, useful information you can act on.

How to build a Malta-ready CV

Put the most relevant experience near the top. Use clear job titles, dates, responsibilities and measurable achievements. Keep formatting simple and readable.

How to spot a suspicious vacancy

Be cautious if someone asks you to pay upfront for a job, requests sensitive identity documents through an untrusted channel, guarantees a job without an interview, or refuses to provide verifiable employer details.

Where to verify Malta vacancies

When possible, check official or original sources. MaltaJobsHub should link out to the employer or official vacancy source rather than copying a listing without verification.

How to apply strategically

Choose a small number of suitable roles, tailor your CV to each one, keep a record of applications, and follow up professionally.
